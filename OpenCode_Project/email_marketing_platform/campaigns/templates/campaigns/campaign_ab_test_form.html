A/B Test Tips
Best Practices
  • Test only ONE element at a time
  • Common things to test: subject lines, CTAs, layout, images
  • Wait for statistical significance before declaring a winner
  • Minimum sample size: 1000 recipients per variant
What to Test
  • Subject Lines: Different wording, length, personalization
  • CTAs: Button color, text, placement
  • Content: Short vs long, different images
  • Sender: Different from names
