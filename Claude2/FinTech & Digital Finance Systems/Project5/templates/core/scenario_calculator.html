How It Works

Compound Interest Formula

This calculator uses the compound interest formula with monthly contributions to project your portfolio value over time.

FV = P(1+r)^n + PMT × [((1+r)^n - 1) / r]

Key Factors

  • Time Horizon: Longer periods significantly increase compound growth
  • Regular Contributions: Consistent investments reduce timing risk
  • Market Conditions: Returns vary based on economic cycles
  • Rate of Return: Based on your risk profile allocation
