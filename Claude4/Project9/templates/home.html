Understanding Soil Parameters

🌱

Nitrogen (N)

Role: Essential for leaf and stem growth, chlorophyll production, and overall plant vigor.

Deficiency: Yellowing leaves, stunted growth

Good Range: 50-90 mg/kg

💪

Phosphorus (P)

Role: Promotes root development, flowering, and seed production. Essential for energy transfer.

Deficiency: Poor root growth, purpling leaves

Good Range: 20-50 mg/kg

🍌

Potassium (K)

Role: Enhances disease resistance, improves fruit quality, and regulates water uptake.

Deficiency: Brown leaf edges, weak stems

Good Range: 30-80 mg/kg

⚗️

pH Level

Role: Measures soil acidity/alkalinity. Affects nutrient availability to plants.

Too Acidic (pH < 5.5): Nutrients get locked

Too Alkaline (pH > 7.5): Reduced nutrient uptake

Ideal: 6.0 - 7.0 for most crops

💧

Moisture

Role: Critical for nutrient transport, photosynthesis, and overall plant metabolism.

Too Low: Wilting, nutrient deficiency

Too High: Root rot, poor aeration

Ideal: 40-80% depending on crop
